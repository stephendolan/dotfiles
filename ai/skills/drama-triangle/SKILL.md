# Drama Triangle

Analyze how the communication assigns agency:

| Drama role | Signal | Empowered stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Victim | helpless or choice-less | Creator: state the desired outcome and owned choices |
| Persecutor | blame or character judgment | Challenger: describe structure and ask for accountable change |
| Rescuer | savior, martyr, or dependency | Coach: respect capability and offer support without taking over |

1. Identify the role assigned to every party, including the author, and note
   rotations between roles.
2. Trace what each assignment accomplishes: avoided responsibility, diminished
   agency, false urgency, dependency, or blame.
3. Reframe the message around the author's honest want, observable structure,
   mutual benefit, and every party's agency.
4. Finish with a one-sentence version that needs no victim, villain, or hero.
